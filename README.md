Quick guides to use;

Clone the repo

Copy this code below to explore chrome extension.

chrome://extensions/


On the extensions page, click on "Load unpacked" 

Select the folder and click open

Move to the extension pane and pin it to use



To scrape emails, use the search strings below. You can change the industry types and location.


"resturant" ("gmail" or "outlook" or "hotmail" or "yahoo" or "icloud" or "protonmail" or "aol" or "email" or "mail") "canada" ("instagram.com" or "facebook.com" or "linkedin.com" or "x.com" or "twitter.com" or "tiktok.com" or "youtube.com" or "pinterest.com" or "threads.net")
